Participant information

Please read this before booking. Booking is not consent. You’ll be asked again in person, and you can still say no.

What is this study about?

We’re looking at how people interact with a speech-driven system, and how they respond when it doesn’t do what they expected. Systems that build things from spoken instructions are becoming common, and very little is known about what people do when one of them gets it wrong.

There is no skill being tested and no right way to talk to it. We are studying the system, not you.

What will I actually do?
  • Answer a few background questions: age range, prior VR use, that sort of thing.
  • Put on a VR headset and see a simple outdoor scene.
  • Six short tasks. Each time we’ll describe something to create or change, and you ask the system for it in your own words. There is no script and no correct phrasing.
  • After each one, we’ll ask what you made of what happened.
  • At the end, a questionnaire and a few minutes of conversation.

You can take the headset off whenever you like, for any reason.

Do I have to take part, and can I change my mind?

Taking part is entirely voluntary. You can stop at any point without giving a reason, and nothing happens as a result. You keep the thank-you payment either way.

You can also withdraw afterwards. Tell us within two weeks and we’ll delete everything from your session. After that point the data has been combined for analysis and individual sessions can no longer be picked out.

Are there any risks?

The main one is simulator sickness: some people feel dizzy or slightly nauseous in VR. The scene is deliberately calm and you stay standing or seated in one place, which makes this much less likely. If you feel unwell at any point, say so and we’ll stop immediately.

The headset is cleaned between participants. If you wear glasses, they normally fit underneath.

What happens to my data?
  • Audio is recorded for the whole session, so we can transcribe what you said to the system and what you told us afterwards.
  • Your session is stored under a participant number, not your name. Nothing in the study data identifies you.
  • Your name and email are used only to arrange the session. They are kept separately from the study data and deleted once the study is finished.
  • Recordings are stored securely and deleted once they have been transcribed.
  • Anonymised data may be published, shared with other researchers, or included in a thesis. Nothing published will identify you.
